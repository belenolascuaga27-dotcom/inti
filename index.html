About us

Why we exist

Inti's purpose is to create a community for those with South American heritage across campus by promoting culture, friendship, and representation.

We want to focus on sharing South American history, cuisine, traditions, and values with anyone interested in learning about this region. We prioritize cultural exchange on the basis of respect and diversity.

You don't need South American roots to join. Come to an event, say hola, and bring a friend.

  • Culture & history

    Sharing the history, traditions, and values of South America with campus.

  • Friendship & cuisine

    Meetups and food nights where members feel at home, from ceviche to empanadas.

  • Representation

    A visible voice for South American students, built on respect and diversity.
